DURMITOR NATIONAL PARK

Black Lake Loop

A peaceful walk through Montenegro's spectacular mountain scenery.

DifficultyEasy
Duration1–2 hours
Distance3.5 km approx.
ElevationLow

About this trail

Black Lake, also known as Crno Jezero, is one of the best-known natural attractions in Durmitor National Park. This trail offers beautiful views of the lake and surrounding forest, making it a popular choice for visitors looking for a relaxed outdoor experience.

Take your time, enjoy the scenery, and follow marked paths to help protect the local environment.

What to bring

  • Comfortable walking shoes
  • Drinking water
  • A light jacket for changing mountain weather
  • A bag to take your rubbish back with you

🌱 Leave no trace

Stay on marked paths, respect wildlife, avoid littering, and leave plants and natural objects undisturbed.

Safety information

Conditions can change with the weather and season. Check local park guidance before setting out, wear suitable footwear, and follow signs and any temporary trail closures.

Note: Distance and duration are approximate. Check current local information before your visit.
